What is StuDEC and what is its core idea?

    StuDEC is the international gathering for students from democratic schools from around the world.
    The core idea behind StuDEC is for us students to exchange knowledge, experience, and our (creative) idea's with regard to democratic education. But most importantly the goal is for us to build connections transcending borders, making democratic education flourish around the world.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions
Where is StuDEC 2027?

StuDEC 2027 takes place on Texel, an island in the Netherlands located in the Waddenzee.

What does “StuDEC” stand for?

Students’ Democratic Education Conference.

Which edition of StuDEC is this?

This is the fourth edition of StuDEC.

Who can participate?

Students from democratic schools and related educational communities can participate. We welcome participants of all ages, although most participants in previous years were between 13 and 19 years old.

Can I come alone?

Students may come on their own, but if the participant is under the age of 18, at least one adult staff member from their democratic school should also attend the event. Adult graduates of democratic schools are also welcome to attend independently.

Adult graduates of democratic schools are also welcome to attend independently.

Will there be adults or staff present?

Each participating democratic school should have at least one Group Leader attending with its students. The number of staff should be appropriate to the size of the group and the individual needs of its members, with at least one staff member per 15 students.

Do I need to bring bedding?

No. The venue provides all bedding.

How are rooms assigned?

Accommodation is provided in gender-specific rooms. Participants can indicate their preferred gender category during registration and can request to stay with people from their school whom they know. After ~150 registrations, the room assignments can be made and students can give their preference. Room arrangements are coordinated with Group Leaders.
